This is a passion project for me — the 1954 Liturgy of the Hours is what has spoken to me the most. I went searching for the books in a parallel format. To no avail, they do not exist. It's either Latin or English, and you'd better have at least $2,000 to buy a set. What I did is take all public domain data and transform it into this. To be the only one of its kind is special, and I want to share it with everyone who is interested. I'd like to grow it into something more in the future, but for now, this is great. Praise our Lord, Jesus Christ, who lives and reigns with us.
